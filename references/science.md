## Hormonal Rhythm (Relative to Wake Time T)

| Window | Morning Lark | Intermediate | Night Owl |
|--------|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| CAR peak | T ~ T+1h | T ~ T+1.5h | T ~ T+2.5h |
| Deep Focus Golden Window | T+1h ~ T+5h | T+1.5h ~ T+6h | T+4h ~ T+9h |
| Energy Trough | T+6h ~ T+8h | T+7h ~ T+9h | T+9h ~ T+11h |
| Afternoon Secondary Peak | T+8h ~ T+11h | T+9h ~ T+12h | T+11h ~ T+14h |
| Memory Consolidation Window | T+12h ~ T+14h | T+13h ~ T+15h | T+15h ~ T+17h |
| Offline Integration | After T+14h | After T+16h | After T+18h |

**Cortisol Awakening Response (CAR):** Cortisol surges 50–160% within 30–45 minutes of waking. This primes the prefrontal cortex for executive function. The CAR peak is when the brain is activating — not yet at full capacity.

**Deep Focus Golden Window:** Cortisol reaches sustained high; BDNF elevated from CAR; prefrontal cortex at peak executive function. This is the only window suitable for the hardest cognitive work.

**Energy Trough:** Melatonin lightly re-enters circulation; core body temperature dips; attention and working memory drop measurably. Forcing deep focus here produces low-quality output and accelerates crash.

**Afternoon Secondary Peak:** Core body temperature reaches daily maximum; reaction time and procedural performance peak. Suitable for skill practice, collaborative work, and creative tasks — not raw analytical deep focus.

**Memory Consolidation Window:** Pre-sleep review leverages overnight memory processing. Spaced repetition material reviewed here has significantly higher retention than material reviewed in the morning.

**Night Owl note:** Peak focus window starts ~3 hours later than Morning Lark. For a Night Owl waking at 8:00, the golden window runs roughly 12:00–17:00. The CAR is also delayed — the first 2.5 hours after waking are low-function regardless of sleep quality. Never schedule hard tasks for Night Owls in the morning.

**Morning exercise bonus:** Aerobic or strength training elevates norepinephrine and BDNF for 2–4 hours post-exercise. Regardless of chronotype, if the user exercised this morning, the Deep Focus Golden Window opens immediately after training ends. Treat block capacity as one tier higher than the sleep score alone suggests.

## Attention Span Science

**The 20–45 minute reality:** Chronic exposure to high-frequency stimulation (notifications, short videos, social media) has reduced most people's effective sustained focus window to 20–45 minutes — well below the theoretical 90-minute ultradian rhythm.

**Attention Residue Effect (Sophie Leroy, 2009):** After any task switch, the brain needs approximately 23 minutes to fully disengage from the previous task and re-engage with the new one. Every interruption, even brief, carries this cognitive cost. This is why batching similar tasks dramatically reduces total cognitive load.

**Progressive stretch strategy:** Starting with shorter focus blocks (25 min) and extending gradually (45 → 60 → 90 min) as the session warms up is more effective than forcing long blocks from the start. The brain's focus capacity is trainable over time.

**Block length by sleep + subjective state:**

| Sleep | Feel | Block Length |
|-------|------|-------------|
| < 6h | Any | 25 min |
| 6–7h | Groggy | 25 min |
| 6–7h | Normal/Sharp | 45 min |
| 7–9h | Groggy | 45 min |
| 7–9h | Normal | 45–60 min |
| 7–9h | Sharp | 60–90 min |
| > 9h | Any | 45 min (oversleep causes sleep inertia) |

## Dopamine Regulation

**Root problem:** High-stimulation content (short videos, social media, gaming) repeatedly spikes dopamine, which raises the baseline over time. Once the baseline is elevated, low-stimulation tasks like reading or coding feel unrewarding by comparison — the brain structurally resists them.

**Dopamine cool-down:** Allowing 60 minutes without high-stimulation input before deep work lets the dopamine baseline naturally fall back to a level where focused work feels rewarding again.

**Delayed reward principle:** Scheduling high-dopamine activities (entertainment, social media) strictly after deep work completion leverages the anticipation of reward to sustain focus motivation during the work block.

**Exercise's special value:** Exercise-induced dopamine release follows a healthy rhythmic pattern and improves receptor sensitivity over time — unlike the spike-crash pattern of digital stimulation. Morning exercise is the best natural dopamine reset available.

**Notification management:** The mere visual presence of a phone — even face-down, even silenced — measurably reduces available working memory and increases cognitive load. During deep work, the phone should be in a different room or inside a bag.

## Task Classification and Time Window Matching

| Task Type | Examples | Best Time Window |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| Deep Focus | Math, coding, writing, analysis | Deep Focus Golden Window |
| Creative / Divergent | Brainstorming, design, discussion | Tail of morning window or afternoon secondary peak |
| Skill Practice | Language speaking, instruments, exercise | Afternoon secondary peak |
| Spaced Repetition / Review | Anki, knowledge review | Memory Consolidation Window (2–3h before sleep) |
| Admin / Mechanical | Email, filing, archiving, forms | Energy trough |
| Relaxation / Light Reading | Journaling, magazines, light podcasts | Offline Integration Period |
| High-Stimulation Entertainment | Social media, games, short videos | Fixed slot only — never adjacent to deep work |
